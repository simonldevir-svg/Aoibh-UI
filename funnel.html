How to read this

Views are page loads, not unique people (the tracking uses no cookies). They should be roughly 70–95% of the clicks Google Ads reports. Much lower usually means a slow page or ad blockers.
Button clicks show which button was used. Briefs started is the brief chat opening; briefs saved means they finished it; deposits and balances are payments confirmed by Stripe.
  • Enough data? Wait for around 100 views per page, or 4 to 6 weeks, before judging a page. One week of €200 is noisy.
  • Few clicks on buttons: the page or the promise isn't landing. Check the section chart to see where people leave.
  • Clicks but few briefs saved: the brief chat is the barrier, or the visitors aren't the right fit.
  • Briefs but no deposits: the price or the matching step is the barrier. Look at the briefs themselves.
  • A keyword with spend but no briefs started after about €40 is a candidate to pause. Add it to the negative keywords if it is clearly the wrong audience.
