QUESTIONS

Questions, answered.

What counts as one project?

A single deliverable: one logo, one set of brand guidelines, one packaging concept, one landing page, and so on. Multi-part work like a full identity system is scoped and quoted separately.

Do I need to sign a contract?

No. The trial is a one-off payment, and the Starter and Growth subscriptions run month to month with no long-term lock-in. You can pause or cancel ahead of your next billing date.

Who actually does the design work?

Both, deliberately. A designer from the network produces the first draft by hand; an art director refines the chosen direction using AI for fast iteration; and every delivery is re-checked by both a human and AI before it ships. A person is always responsible for the actual craft.

What if I want changes?

The trial includes 1 round of revisions and Starter includes up to 3 per project. If you need more, an additional round can be added to that project.

How fast will I get my first draft?

Two days is the standard turnaround from an approved brief to first drafts. Growth and Enterprise plans get priority turnaround on top of that.

Can I trial Aoibh if I'm already a client?

The Trial Project is for new clients only. Existing clients can move straight to a subscription.

My project needs real functionality, not just design. Can you build that too?

Subscriptions cover design. Anything requiring functionality, such as ecommerce integration, custom development or backend logic, sits outside subscription scope and is quoted separately.
