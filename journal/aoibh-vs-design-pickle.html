Aoibh vs. Design Pickle: what to actually look for in a design subscription

Aoibh Journal6 min read

If you're comparing Aoibh to Design Pickle, Superside, or another flat-fee design subscription, the specific pricing and features of any one provider will change over time — which makes them a shaky basis for a decision. What doesn't change is the list of questions worth asking about any subscription design service before you commit a monthly fee to one. Here's that list, and how Aoibh answers each one.

What every design subscription should answer

Who actually reviews the work?

Some subscriptions route requests straight from a generator to your inbox. Others put a person between the AI and the delivery. That distinction matters more than almost anything else on this list, since it's the difference between "technically finished" and "actually good."

How is the designer matched to your brief?

A shared pool of generalists is a different experience than a match made specifically for your project's category and style. Ask whether matching is based on availability (whoever's free next) or fit (whoever's actually right for this brief).

What's the real revision policy?

"Unlimited revisions" and "unlimited requests" are not the same claim, and the difference is usually in the fine print. Ask specifically how many revision rounds a single project gets before it's treated as a new request.

Can you see where your project actually stands?

A status update you have to ask for isn't a status update. Look for a real, live view of where a project is — matched, in progress, in review, delivered — rather than relying on a shared inbox or a Slack channel you have to check yourself.

What happens if you want to stop?

Month-to-month with no long-term lock-in is a meaningfully different commitment than a quarterly or annual contract. Ask exactly when a cancellation takes effect, and whether you're billed again before it does.

How Aoibh answers these

Every plan runs on the same system, whether it's the Starter tier or Enterprise: a designer is matched from a vetted global network at 80%+ confidence before any work starts — not whoever happens to be free. Every delivery is re-checked by a human art director and AI before it reaches you, regardless of how long a designer has been in the network. The average time to a first draft is two days, tracked in a live dashboard you can check yourself rather than asking for a status update. Subscriptions run month to month with no long-term lock-in, and cancelling takes effect ahead of your next billing date — not immediately mid-cycle, and not after one more forced payment.

None of that is a claim about what any other provider does or doesn't do — it's just what's actually true about how Aoibh works, and it's the same list of questions worth asking whichever service you end up comparing it to.
